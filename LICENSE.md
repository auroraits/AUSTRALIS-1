## Important compatibility note

This repository is source-available under non-commercial terms. It is not
"open source" in the OSI sense, because the commercial-use restriction is a
field-of-use restriction. The Open Source Definition requires free redistribution
and no discrimination against fields of endeavor.

Reference: https://opensource.org/osd
